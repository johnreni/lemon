Privacy policy

This page explains what personal information LEMON collects through getlemondigital.com, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

LEMON is a digital studio founded by Fowe Douliak (John Reni), with offices in Douala (Cameroon), Brussels (Belgium) and Abu Dhabi (United Arab Emirates). For any privacy question, email info@getlemondigital.com.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us. If you contact us through the form, by email or on WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to share: usually your name, email address or phone number, the service you need and your message.
  • Technical data. Like every website, our hosting provider automatically processes your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

We do not use advertising trackers, and this website does not set cookies.

How the contact form works

The form does not store anything on our website. When you press Send on WhatsApp, it opens WhatsApp (a service of Meta) with your message written for you. When you press Send by email, it either opens your own email app or sends your message to us through a form service that delivers it to our inbox.

Why we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare your free audit and send you a quote (taking steps at your request before a contract).
  • To work with you if you become a client (performing our contract).
  • To keep the website running and secure (our legitimate interest).

We never sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who else handles it

  • GitHub (hosting of this website) and, where used, Cloudflare (security and delivery). They may process technical data in other countries under their own safeguards.
  • WhatsApp / Meta, if you choose to contact us there.
  • Our email provider, and a form-delivery service if the form is set up to use one.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages only as long as needed to answer you and, if you become a client, to manage our work together and meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it. Email info@getlemondigital.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are in the European Union and are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority — in Belgium, the Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page.
